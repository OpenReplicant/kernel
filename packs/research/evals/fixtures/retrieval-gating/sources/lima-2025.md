# Retrieval-Gated Decoding Reduces Hallucination in Abstractive Summarization

Ana Lima, Ben Okafor. 2025. Synthetic Proceedings of Summarization 12. doi:10.5555/wmk.rgd.2025

## Abstract

Abstractive summarizers often state facts that are not in the source document. We introduce retrieval-gated decoding (RGD), which blocks a token when no retrieved passage of the source supports it. On the NewsSum-2 benchmark, RGD raises factual consistency from 71% to 84%, while ROUGE-L drops by 0.4 points. We hypothesize that gating at the token level, rather than the sentence level, drives the gain.
