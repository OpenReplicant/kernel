# Does Retrieval Gating Help Long-Document Summarization?

Chen Wu. 2026. Synthetic Journal of Text Generation 4. doi:10.5555/wmk.rgd.2026a

## Abstract

Retrieval-gated decoding (RGD; Lima and Okafor, 2025) was reported to improve the factual consistency of news summaries. We test RGD on GovLong, a benchmark of long government reports. RGD did not improve factual consistency on GovLong (78% with gating, 77% without). We also introduce sentence-level gating, a variant of RGD that gates whole sentences: it scored the same as token-level gating, which suggests that the gain reported on news does not come from gating at the token level.
