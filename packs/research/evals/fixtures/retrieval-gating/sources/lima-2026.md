# Retrieval-Gated Decoding for Clinical Note Summarization

Ana Lima, Dev Patel. 2026. Synthetic Workshop on Clinical Text 3. doi:10.5555/wmk.rgd.2026b

## Abstract

We apply retrieval-gated decoding to summaries of clinical notes. On the MedNotes corpus, RGD raised factual consistency from 64% to 79%, in line with earlier results on news summaries. Our code is released under the MIT license. Whether RGD improves factual consistency on documents longer than 4,000 tokens remains an open question.
