# Invoice approval at Acme Tools

Acme Tools pays supplier invoices through one invoice approval process, owned by the Finance department.

## Steps

1. Accounts payable receives supplier invoices by email.
2. An AP clerk enters each invoice into the ERP system.
3. The clerk checks the invoice against its purchase order.
4. If the amounts match, the invoice goes to the invoice approver. If they do not match, it is returned to the supplier.
5. The invoice approver approves the invoice.
6. Finance pays approved invoices in the Friday payment run. Only approved invoices may be paid.
