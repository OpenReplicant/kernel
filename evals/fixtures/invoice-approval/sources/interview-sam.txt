Interview with Sam Ortiz, Finance, 14 September 2026

Interviewer: Who approves supplier invoices today?
Sam: I do. I took over invoice approval from Dana at the start of March 2026, when she moved to the treasury team.

Interviewer: Does every invoice get approved before payment?
Sam: Not really. Invoices under 500 euros are often paid without any approval.

Interviewer: How do you feel about the process?
Sam: Honestly, it feels slow, and nobody likes the ERP screens.
